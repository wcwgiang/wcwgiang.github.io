I'm a UX researcher and human factors specialist with 15+ years studying how people understand, learn, and safely use emerging technologies in regulated, high-stakes environments, spanning consumer finance, healthcare, and transportation. My work pairs rigorous mixed-methods research with usability evaluation and human-centered design.

I specialize in inclusive, accessible research approaches that support diverse user populations, and in translating evidence into practical guidance for design, policy, and consumer education. After leading interdisciplinary research as a professor, I'm now applying that depth to product and UX research in industry.

Selected Research

Choose a theme to show related publications below.

Vehicle automation training & education

Designing and evaluating training that helps consumers understand in-vehicle automation, comparing scaffolding strategies across older, younger, and student drivers via simulator and eye-tracking studies.

Health-insurance decision-making

Mixed-methods research on consumer comprehension during enrollment: how decision-aid type, information structure, and framing shape choices in regulated consumer contexts.

Uncertainty & decision support

Contextual research plus controlled and online experiments on how people interpret uncertainty in time-critical decision support, from medical dispatch to uncertainty visualization. Outcomes informed technology transfer of a decision-support tool to a computer-aided dispatch vendor.

Inclusive design for vulnerable drivers

Participatory design with older adults and people with mild cognitive impairment, and on-road clinical studies of driver-assistance for people with Parkinson's disease.

DOT Inclusive Design Challenge ↗ Open dataset (ICPSR) ↗

Distraction, attention & wearables

How smartphones and wearables reshape attention and safety: distracted walking, notification engagement while driving, and risk perception across environments.
